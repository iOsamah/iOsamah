## ⭐ Featured Project — Telco Customer Churn

<table>
<tr>
<td width="55%" valign="top">

**Problem:** A telecom company loses **26.5%** of its 7,043 customers. Who is leaving, and why?

**Approach:** Cleaned the data, engineered a churn flag, and compared churn rates across contract, payment, service, and demographic segments in a six-panel dashboard.

**Findings:**
- Month-to-month contracts churn at **42.7%** vs **2.8%** on two-year contracts
- Customers without Tech Support churn at **41.6%** vs **15.2%** with it
- Fiber optic customers churn at **41.9%** despite paying more

**Recommendation:** Push month-to-month customers to annual plans and bundle Tech Support for high-risk segments.
